Case Study 01 · Branding + Web

Northlight Capital — a rebrand that rebuilt trust

The Challenge

Northlight had grown through acquisition, and its identity showed it — a patchwork of logos, tones, and site experiences. Institutional buyers loved the strategy; the brand kept getting in the way. We were asked to unify the story and rebuild the digital flagship in one quarter.

The Approach

We repositioned Northlight around a single promise — "Clarity for complex capital." A restrained identity system, a custom design language, and a rebuilt site with research reports, thought leadership, and a transactional client portal shaped around that promise.

Organic traffic in six months

2.4x

Increase in qualified inquiries

Case Study 02 · Branding

Mono Coffee Co. — identity for a craft brand

The Challenge

A single-origin roaster with beautiful beans and a forgettable package — lost on crowded specialty shelves. Mono needed an identity that felt as considered as its roasting, without shouting.

The Approach

We built a monochrome identity around texture, typography, and restraint: a logotype set in a quiet grotesque, packaging structured like a tasting card, and a tagline system that works across bags, cups, and social.
